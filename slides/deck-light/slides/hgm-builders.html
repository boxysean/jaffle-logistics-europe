You don't, by hand. dbt_context_engineering ships three skills for coding agents, in the repo's skills folder. dbt-ce-setup installs the package and sets the vars, including the spend gate that keeps AI functions off until you turn them on. dbt-ce-pipeline knows the pipeline: split, chunk, an incremental embed that only pays for changed text, the knowledge base, vector search, and the cost guards. dbt-ce-ai-functions knows classify and extract, with versioned prompts and schemas. You ask for the source you want added; the agent loads the skills and writes the models, the YAML and the tests in one pull request. Your job is the review. The evals slide is how to make that review less of a judgement call.
