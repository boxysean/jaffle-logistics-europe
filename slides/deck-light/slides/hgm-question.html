In Vienna I finished by walking backwards in dbt Platform, from search and knowledge_base to the source. This section is that walk, slowed down. One question: what happened in 2025 Q1 that caused on-time to go down? An agent gets it and plans two lookups, one for the number and one for the cause. It reaches the warehouse through MCP tools. The number comes from the semantic layer and the cause from the knowledge base, and both are dbt models built from the source systems. One layer per slide. Every number and every row on the next slides is real, read from the production tables.
