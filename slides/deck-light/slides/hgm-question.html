In a live demo this is a walk backwards in dbt Platform, from search and knowledge_base to the source. This section is that walk turned around and slowed down. It reads like dbt lineage, sources on the left and the agent on the right, and we go left to right: the sources, the models, the two truths they serve, the tools, then the agent. One question: what happened in 2025 Q1 that caused on-time to go down? An agent gets it and plans two lookups, one for the number and one for the cause. It reaches the warehouse through MCP tools. The number comes from the semantic layer and the cause from the knowledge base, and both are dbt models built from the source systems. One layer per slide; the bar at the top of each one shows where we are. Every number and every row on the next slides is real, read from the production tables.
