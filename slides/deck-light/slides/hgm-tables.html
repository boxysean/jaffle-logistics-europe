What the two tools actually read. Left, the semantic layer: on_time_rate is a ratio of on_time_shipments over shipments, and on_time_shipments sums fct_shipments.is_ontime, which is true when the status is delivered. Nobody restates that definition in a prompt; the agent asks for the metric by name. Right, one real row of knowledge_base, out of 2,708. It is the first of three passages cut from IR-9001. It carries its source type, the account it belongs to (CLI-0042, Jaffle Werke), the passage text, its embedding (1,024 numbers from databricks-gte-large-en, the first three shown), the time the report was filed, a citation back to the report, and a classification label. The embedding is what the vector search compares against; the label is what the filter on the previous slide used. The next two slides show how that row gets made.
