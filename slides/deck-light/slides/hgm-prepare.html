This is the slowed-down part. One click per model. One: the incident system exports the report with its line breaks escaped, and stg_incident_reports puts them back. Two: int_incident_reports_documents joins the report to its shipment, which gives it an account, CLI-0042 Jaffle Werke, and a citation URL back to IR-9001. Three: split_incident_reports, the package's split_sentences macro, cuts it into 13 sentences. Four: chunk_incident_reports packs those sentences into passages near the 100-token target: 6 sentences, 6 sentences, and the last one on its own, "Reviewed with dispatch." A passage never spans two reports. Five: incident_reports_hashed takes a SHA-256 of each passage's text. That fingerprint is what makes the next step cheap. None of these five calls an AI function. They are SQL, they are tested, and they give the same rows every run.
