Step three, embed. Each passage goes to an embedding model, here Databricks' gte-large-en, and comes back as 1,024 numbers. These bars are the first 48 of them, the real values: amber above the line, teal below. Nobody reads these numbers; what matters is that two passages about the same thing get numbers that sit close together. That is how search works: the question is embedded with the same model, and the nearest passages win. For the Paris storm question, IR-9001's first passage is nearest, at 0.779. Note the third passage, "Reviewed with dispatch.": a different shape, because it means something different. Each passage also carries a hash of its text. The embed model is incremental on that hash: next build, unchanged text is not sent, or paid for, again. Only a change of embedding model re-embeds everything.
