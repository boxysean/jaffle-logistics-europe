Four steps, and the shape of the data after each. One incident report comes in as a file. Parse turns the file into text: one document. Chunk cuts the text into passages that fit an embedding model: three. Embed turns each passage into a vector, and classify gives each passage a label, side by side: three vectors and three labels. Then the knowledge base unions those three rows with every other source, 2,708 rows in all. Parse, embed and classify call an AI function and cost money; chunking is plain SQL.
