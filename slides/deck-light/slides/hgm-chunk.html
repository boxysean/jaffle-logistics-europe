Step two, chunk. A whole report is too long and too mixed to embed as one thing, so it is cut down. split_incident_reports cuts it at sentence boundaries: 13 sentences, from the header line to "Reviewed with dispatch." chunk_incident_reports packs consecutive sentences into passages near a 100-token target, and never across two reports: sentences 1 to 6 are the first passage, the summary of the storm and the jackknife; 7 to 12 the second, the timeline, root cause and remediation; and the last sentence is left on its own. Each passage remembers which sentences went into it, so it can be traced back. This step is plain SQL, no AI, and it gives the same passages every run.
