Two calls, one per source of truth. Left, the number: query_metrics on the governed on_time_rate metric by quarter. Q1 is the low one at 90.33%, against 95.40, 95.12 and 92.62. The agent asks again for Q1 by origin city and Paris stands out: 56.60% in January, on 53 shipments; Berlin is next at 80.65 and the other three hubs are at 91.67 or better. Right, the cause: a vector search over knowledge_base, the query "truck accident during the Paris winter storm", filtered to the weather_disruption label. The top passage, at a similarity of 0.779, is IR-9001: the incident report for the semi that jackknifed leaving the Paris hub on 15 January. These are the real rows, from the search model in last night's build. The agent that strings the two calls together is a design, not a running system; the tools and the tables under it are real.
